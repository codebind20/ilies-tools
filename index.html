About ELIAS TOOLS

ELIAS TOOLS is an independent project that gathers the small utilities people need every day into one clean, fast and privacy-minded workspace. It starts with the most-used PDF, image, calculator, text, developer and security tools, then grows toward AI, productivity and professional workflows.

Language support matters: the AI tools help with English, French, German and Arabic, so the toolkit works for an international audience.

Frequently asked questions

Is ELIAS TOOLS really free?

Yes. The tools are free to use and the essentials need no account.

Are my files uploaded?

Whenever a task can run in your browser, it stays there. Files are only uploaded when a tool genuinely needs it, and AI tools send the text you enter to the AI service to generate a result.

What does the Soon label mean?

Some PDF tools and the currency converter are still being built. They are labelled Soon and go live as they are released.

Which AI tools are included?

Writing, summarizing, translating and proofreading, CV and cover-letter help, French and German practice, an Arabic assistant, and developer helpers, powered by Claude AI.

Can I request a new tool?

Yes. Send your idea through the contact section and it will be considered for the roadmap.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.

Questions, feedback or a tool idea?

We read every message. Tell us what would make your day easier.
